Home

This is an archive of the life and theatre work of Mechthild Harkness (1923–1986), her husband Alan Harkness, and the companies and studios they were part of — Ab-Intra Studio Theatre, the Chekhov Theatre Studio, High Valley Theatre, and The Harkness Studio.

This site was rescued from a “wetpaint” wiki and moved to GitHub as a work in progress. Contributions are welcome — send a pull request.
